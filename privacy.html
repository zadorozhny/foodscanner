Privacy Policy

How Orange handles your information.

Last updated October 14, 2026

Camera & history

Orange processes camera frames on your device to read barcodes; frames are never saved or transmitted. Scan history stays on your device until you delete it or remove the app and its data. Swipe left on an entry to delete it. Your device backups may include app data, depending on your settings.

Product lookups

Orange sends the scanned barcode to Open Food Facts. We do not include your name, email, history, or camera images. The service also receives standard connection information, such as your IP address, under its privacy policy.

Payments & Apple services

Apple handles purchases and payments. Orange uses StoreKit subscription status to unlock Premium and never receives your payment details. Optional App Store ratings and reviews are also handled by Apple. See Apple’s privacy policy.

No accounts or tracking

Orange has no accounts, analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or third-party tracking. We do not sell personal information. These pages use no analytics or browser storage; the website host may process technical requests for delivery and security.

Support emails

If you contact us, we use your email address and message to help you. Email is handled by Gmail under Google’s privacy policy. Correspondence is kept only as needed for support and legal obligations.

Your choices

Control camera access in iOS Settings and delete history in Orange. We cannot access your local history. Contact hellologme@gmail.com to request access, correction, or deletion of information you sent us. Other privacy rights, including contacting a regulator, may apply where you live. If a child sends us personal information, contact us so we can address it.

About this policy

This policy covers Orange and its support pages. “We” means the developer of Orange, reachable at hellologme@gmail.com. We will update the date above when this policy changes and provide notice of material changes as appropriate.
