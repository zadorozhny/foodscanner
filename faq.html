Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about scanning, scores, and Premium.

How do I scan a product?+

Tap Scan Barcode and point the camera at the barcode. Allow camera access and connect to the internet for the lookup.

Why isn’t a product found?+

It may not be in Open Food Facts, the community database Orange uses. Some records are incomplete or outdated. Always check the package.

What does the score mean?+

Orange combines Nutri-Score and NOVA processing data into a 0–100 score. Nutrition amounts are per 100 g. Eco-Score is a separate environmental rating. These are general indicators, not medical or dietary advice.

Is Orange safe to use for allergies?+

Always check the package’s ingredients and allergen warnings. Database information may be incomplete. Consult a qualified health professional before making dietary changes.

What is included for free and with Premium?+

Your first 3 scans are free; the fourth attempt shows the paywall. Premium unlocks unlimited scans for $24.99/year or $5.99/month. Local prices and currency appear in Apple’s purchase screen. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled.

How do I cancel or request a refund?+

Cancel in Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Orange. Deleting the app does not cancel Premium. Cancellation help · Request a refund from Apple.

How do I restore Premium?+

Tap Restore Purchases on the paywall using the Apple Account that made the purchase.

How do I delete a scan?+

In your history, swipe left on an item and tap Delete. History is stored on your device only.

What happens to my camera images and data?+

Camera frames stay on-device and are never saved or sent. Only the barcode is sent for product lookup, alongside normal connection information. Orange has no accounts, analytics, or tracking. See our Privacy Policy.
