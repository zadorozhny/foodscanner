Free scans & Premium

The first 3 scans are free; the fourth attempt shows a paywall. Free scans do not start a subscription. Premium unlocks unlimited scans for $24.99/year or $5.99/month. Apple’s purchase screen shows the applicable local price, currency, and taxes. Payment is charged to your Apple Account on confirmation.

Use Restore Purchases with the purchasing Apple Account to restore eligible access. Cancellation help · Apple refund requests.
